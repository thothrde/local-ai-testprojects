Corrections & notices

13 October 2026 · P3DH comparison date: The public static edition could previously place two banks’ own-latest P3DH values from different dates side by side as “exactly comparable”; in larger cohorts, statistics could even be computed across those mixed dates. P3DH comparisons now use the latest common date of all selected institutions and withhold statistics/ranking. This moves 523 pair cases onto the common date; two additional P3DH pairs with no common date were previously misclassified as “exactly comparable” and are now NOT_COMPARABLE. In total, 525 pair outcomes change. No data values were changed.

6 October 2026 · scope correction: The introduction could previously be read as if procurement, network, enforcement, project/funding and full hypothesis/counterevidence case functions were already part of this public static edition. An explicit scope notice now separates the published banking/insurance evidence base from local or planned research layers. This correction does not refresh the public core data.
