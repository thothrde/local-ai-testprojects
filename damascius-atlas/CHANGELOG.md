# Changelog

## v1.2.0 — Geographical and editorial expansion (2026-08-06)

### Atlas
- Replaced the schematic point field with an actual geographical SVG atlas.
- Added public-domain Natural Earth land/coastline geometry.
- Added approximate coordinates for all 18 modelled places.
- Added a regional eastern-Mediterranean view and a wide view reaching India.
- Added a gazetteer, graticule, sea and macro-region labels, route filters, uncertainty labels, and clickable route/place details.
- Modern political borders are deliberately omitted; modern coastlines are used only for orientation.

### Bilingual audit
- Fixed the story-modal regression that displayed English scenes while German was selected.
- Audited all 13 stories and all nested scenes in both languages.
- Audited all 8 cases, 26 people, 18 places, 20 timeline entries, 18 paradox cards, 24 fragment cards, 16 glossary entries, 8 sources, and 11 routes.
- Added bilingual personal names where needed, dates/periods, confidence labels, place periods, source dates, and source labels.
- Corrected inherited source labels: Hypatia’s public-authority and murder scenes now cite PH 43E; the Arabian Styx materials cite PH 135.

### Richer content and design
- Added source-critical significance and methodology notes to every narrative path.
- Expanded person panels with dates, places, stories, fragments, relationships, and source basis.
- Expanded place and route panels with coordinates, geographic context, related people, stories, and route certainty.
- Added richer fragment detail modals and full relationship listings.
- Added distinct original SVG/CSS artwork for narrative paths and a map-based hero image.
- Added a substantially expanded introductory essay and three present-day relevance panels.

### Publication safeguards
- No PDF, page image, modern translation, commentary, or critical apparatus is bundled.
- No external runtime assets, tracking, analytics, remote fonts, or tile servers.
- Clarified that the rights notice is copyright-conscious, not a blanket legal guarantee.
- Natural Earth geometry is identified separately as public domain.
